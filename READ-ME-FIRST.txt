YOUR EASY-EDIT PORTFOLIO
========================

1. Right-click the ZIP > Extract All > Extract.
2. Double-click START-HERE.html to preview in Edge or Chrome.

TO ADD YOUR OWN VIDEO
---------------------
1. Copy your MP4 to: assets/videos/skincare-ad.mp4
2. Copy its thumbnail to: assets/thumbnails/skincare-ad.jpg
3. Right-click portfolio.ini > Open with > Notepad.
4. Paste this block at the bottom, on new lines:

[project:skincare-ad]
title=My skincare AI UGC ad
category=ugc
description=Describe the real project and your contribution here.
video=skincare-ad.mp4
thumbnail=skincare-ad.jpg
placeholder=no
tags=9:16 | AI UGC

5. Save portfolio.ini with Ctrl+S.
6. Double-click UPDATE-PORTFOLIO.cmd and read its result.
7. Open START-HERE.html again, or refresh it in your browser.

No JavaScript editing, Python, Node, or installation is needed on Windows 10/11.
The updater uses the PowerShell included with Windows. It refreshes config-data.js
and START-HERE.html. Do not edit those generated files yourself.

Use only filenames after video= and thumbnail=, not full folder paths.
Use lowercase names with hyphens, and match filenames exactly.
Other categories: product or animation.
To add another project, copy the block and give it a unique project ID/filenames.
Example: [project:headphone-ad], headphone-ad.mp4, headphone-ad.jpg.

PERSONAL DETAILS
----------------
Edit name, email, about, skills, and tools under [profile] in portfolio.ini.
Use | between tags, skills, or tools. Keep descriptions on one line.
To hide an existing concept, add enabled=no anywhere within its project block,
before the next heading. To show it again, change that to enabled=yes.

YOUR LIVE WEBSITE
-----------------
index.html is the GitHub Pages homepage. Upload portfolio.ini and your media along
with the website files. The published homepage reads portfolio.ini directly, so
future live-site changes only need the changed INI file and any new media files.
For double-click local previews, run UPDATE-PORTFOLIO.cmd after each change.

Keep START-HERE.html next to the assets folder: actual MP4 videos are not embedded.
The form prepares a brief; it does not automatically send messages.
Your thirteen available video uploads are integrated. Original concept entries are retained but hidden.
Your profile photo, email, WhatsApp and Discord username are included.

See EDITING-GUIDE.md for the filename table, templates, and exact upload steps.
